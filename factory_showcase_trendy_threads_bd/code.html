Precision Mastery

Knitting & Dyeing

Our circular knitting facility produces over 20 tons of premium jersey and interlock daily. Paired with our state-of-the-art dyeing lab, we ensure color consistency and fabric longevity through Swiss-engineered technology.

The Assembly Line

Cutting & Sewing

Precision starts at the cutting table. With automated CAD/CAM spreading and cutting systems, we minimize waste and maximize structural integrity before garments reach our 40 sewing lines.

Artistic Finishes

Printing & Embroidery

Elevate designs with our high-definition screen printing and multi-head Tajima embroidery. From reactive dyes to high-density puff prints, our embellishment unit brings tactile depth to every piece.

The Standard

Quality Control Lab

Our AQL 1.5 standard is maintained through rigorous testing. Shrinkage, pilling, wash-fastness, and tensile strength are monitored in our internal laboratory to ensure global compliance.
